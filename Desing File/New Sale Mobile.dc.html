What changed vs. current screen
Footer collapses. The summary card took ~40% of the screen. Closed keyboard: Grand Total + received/return line, breakdown behind a tap. Open keyboard: one 60px bar with Total, change and Save, sitting above the keyboard.
One scroll surface. Header stays fixed, form scrolls between header and footer. The scroll area shrinks when the keyboard opens and the focused field is scrolled into view with its label.
Cash return inline. Change / short amount is shown next to the Received Cash label so it's visible while typing. Quick amounts (exact, next 500/1000) reduce typing.
Back + Done. Back closes the keyboard first. A Done pill in the header and Next/Done keys move through fields.
Compact stock line. Available and Low Stock warning wrap on one flowing line.
